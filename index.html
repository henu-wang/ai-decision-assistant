5 Decision Prompt Templates

Copy any template, paste into ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini, and fill in your specific situation. Each template is designed to produce structured, actionable analysis.

💼

1. Career & Life Decision Analyzer

Best for: Job offers, career changes, relocation, major life transitions

I'm facing a major decision and need structured analysis. **My situation:** [Describe your current situation in 2-3 sentences] **Option A:** [Describe first option] **Option B:** [Describe second option] **Option C (if any):** [Describe third option or "status quo"] **What matters most to me:** - [Priority 1, e.g., financial security] - [Priority 2, e.g., work-life balance] - [Priority 3, e.g., growth potential] - [Priority 4, e.g., location/lifestyle] **My timeline:** [When do I need to decide?] Please analyze this decision by: 1. Rating each option against my priorities (1-10 scale with explanation) 2. Identifying the biggest risk for each option 3. Telling me what I might be overlooking (blind spots) 4. Applying the "10-10-10 rule": How will I feel about each choice in 10 minutes, 10 months, and 10 years? 5. Giving me a clear recommendation with reasoning Be direct and honest, even if the answer isn't what I want to hear.
📈

2. Investment Decision Framework

Best for: Stock picks, portfolio allocation, real estate, startup investing

Act as a rational investment analyst combining Warren Buffett's value approach with Charlie Munger's mental models. **Investment under consideration:** [What are you thinking of investing in?] **Amount:** [How much?] **Time horizon:** [How long can you hold?] **Risk tolerance:** [Conservative / Moderate / Aggressive] **Current portfolio context:** [Brief description of existing investments] Please analyze using this framework: **Buffett's Checklist:** - Do I understand this business/asset? (Circle of Competence) - Does it have a durable competitive advantage? (Moat) - Is management trustworthy and capable? - Is the price reasonable relative to intrinsic value? (Margin of Safety) **Risk Analysis:** - What's the worst-case scenario and can I survive it? - What are the key assumptions, and what if they're wrong? - What's the opportunity cost vs. index investing? **Munger's Inversion:** - How could this investment go to zero? - What would make me sell at a loss? **Decision:** - Clear buy/hold/pass recommendation with confidence level (%) - The single most important factor in this decision - What additional information would change your analysis Reference: Practice investment decisions at https://keeprule.com/en/

3. Business Strategy Decision Matrix

Best for: Product launches, market entry, pricing, hiring, partnerships

I need to make a strategic business decision. Help me think through it rigorously. **Decision:** [What specifically needs to be decided?] **Context:** [Industry, company stage, competitive landscape] **Options on the table:** 1. [Option 1] 2. [Option 2] 3. [Option 3 / Do nothing] **Constraints:** - Budget: [Available resources] - Timeline: [Deadline or urgency] - Team: [Available people/skills] Please provide: 1. **Pre-Mortem Analysis**: Imagine it's 1 year from now and this decision failed spectacularly. What went wrong? (Do this for each option) 2. **Second-Order Effects**: What are the indirect consequences of each option that I might not be thinking about? 3. **Reversibility Score**: Rate each option 1-10 on how easily it can be reversed if it doesn't work out. 4. **Expected Value Estimate**: Best case, worst case, and most likely case for each option with rough probabilities. 5. **The "Hell Yes" Test**: Which option, if any, makes me excited rather than just acceptable? 6. **Recommendation**: Your top choice with the key deciding factor. For more business decision frameworks, visit https://keeprule.com/en/

4. Ethical Dilemma Navigator

Best for: Moral decisions, conflicts of interest, whistleblowing, fairness issues

I'm facing an ethical dilemma and want to think through it from multiple moral perspectives. **The situation:** [Describe the dilemma in detail] **The stakeholders:** [Who is affected and how?] **My role:** [Your position and responsibilities] **What makes this hard:** [Why simple answers don't work] Please analyze through these ethical lenses: 1. **Utilitarian**: What action produces the greatest good for the greatest number? Quantify the harm and benefit to each stakeholder. 2. **Deontological (Kant)**: What if everyone in my position did the same thing? Is any option treating people merely as means to an end? 3. **Virtue Ethics**: What would the person I most admire do? What character traits does each option reflect? 4. **Care Ethics**: How does each option affect my relationships and responsibilities to those who depend on me? 5. **Newspaper Test**: Would I be comfortable if this decision appeared on the front page tomorrow? 6. **Future Self Test**: Will I be proud of this decision in 5 years? Then give me: - The option that best satisfies the most ethical frameworks - The key trade-off I need to accept - A specific action plan Explore ethical decision scenarios: https://keeprule.com/en/

5. Rapid Decision Optimizer

Best for: Quick everyday decisions, overcoming analysis paralysis, time-sensitive choices

I need to make a quick decision and I'm overthinking it. Help me cut through the noise. **Decision:** [What I need to decide] **Options:** [List 2-4 options briefly] **Deadline:** [When I need to decide] **What's holding me back:** [Why I'm stuck] Apply these rapid decision tools: 1. **The 2-Minute Rule**: If this decision is easily reversible, just pick the option that feels 51% right and move on. Is this decision reversible? (Yes/No) 2. **Regret Minimization**: Which option will I regret NOT trying when I'm 80 years old? 3. **Energy Test**: Which option gives me energy vs. drains it when I think about doing it tomorrow morning? 4. **Coin Flip Test**: I'm flipping a coin - heads is Option A, tails is Option B. It lands on [pick one]. Am I relieved or disappointed? That feeling IS my answer. 5. **One-Line Answer**: In one sentence, what should I do and why? Don't overthink this. Give me a clear, direct answer. For more decision-making practice: https://keeprule.com/en/

Pro Tips for AI-Assisted Decisions

Give Rich Context

The more specific detail you provide about your situation, constraints, and values, the better the AI's analysis. Vague inputs produce vague outputs.

Ask for Counterarguments

After getting a recommendation, ask: "Now argue the opposite position as strongly as you can." This prevents confirmation bias.

Use Multiple Models

Run the same prompt through ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Where they agree, you can be more confident. Where they disagree, investigate further.

Iterate, Don't Accept First Output

Challenge the AI's reasoning. Ask "Why?" and "What if that assumption is wrong?" Push back until the analysis feels genuinely useful.

Combine with Human Wisdom

Use AI analysis as one input alongside advice from trusted people, your own experience, and platforms like KeepRule.

Document Your Decisions

Save the AI conversation. Review it in 6 months. This builds your personal decision-making track record and calibration over time.
